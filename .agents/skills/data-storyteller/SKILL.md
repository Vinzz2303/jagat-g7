# Data Storyteller Skill

When presenting data or charts:
1. **Never Just Show Data**: Always accompany charts with an "AI Insight" or "Executive Summary" sentence that explains the *meaning* of the data.
2. **Action-Oriented**: Data must lead to an action. (e.g., "Tunggakan di Cikande tinggi, rekomendasikan operasi yustisi bulan ini").
3. **Typography for Data**: Use large, bold typography for key metrics, and subdued colors for secondary axes to guide the user's eye to the most important numbers.
