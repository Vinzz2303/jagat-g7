# AI Conversationalist Skill

When simulating AI chat interfaces (like WhatsApp or a Chatbot):
1. **Tone**: The AI must sound highly professional, slightly authoritative, yet perfectly polite (e.g., "Yth. Pimpinan PT Cikande, sistem kami mendeteksi keterlambatan...").
2. **Data-Driven Dialogue**: AI should inject real numbers into the chat (e.g., "Tunggakan senilai Rp 85.5 M").
3. **Visual Realism**: Use staggered timing/animations (typing indicators) if possible, and differentiate between AI messages (clean, automated look) and User replies.
