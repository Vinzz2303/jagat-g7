# Chart Master Skill

1. **Gradients over Solid Colors**: Use SVG `<defs>` and `<linearGradient>` in Recharts to make area/bar charts look premium.
2. **Interactive Tooltips**: Tooltips should never just show raw numbers; they should be styled beautifully and provide context.
3. **Responsive Container**: Always wrap charts in `ResponsiveContainer` to ensure they scale on all devices.
